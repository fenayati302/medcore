Sage — AI Receptionist

An AI phone receptionist for real estate agents. Answers every call, qualifies buyers and sellers, checks the agent's calendar, and books property tours automatically — 24/7.

Melia — AI Receptionist

An AI phone receptionist for general contractors. Answers every call, qualifies new projects and service requests, and books estimates and site visits automatically — 24/7.

SectorIQ

A live market-intelligence platform that maps every business in your territory, scores each one for fit, and alerts you when a watchlisted account changes. Built first for health and care — hundreds of thousands of labeled provider records across 61 classifications and 16 buyer types — so sales and partnership teams stop guessing who to call and start working a ranked, always-current pipeline.

CaptureVantage

Federal opportunity intelligence that tells contractors what to pursue, what to avoid, and why — before a dollar is spent on a proposal. Built for firms from $5M to $100M+, it qualifies and prioritizes contract opportunities so capture teams win the right work instead of chasing every RFP.
